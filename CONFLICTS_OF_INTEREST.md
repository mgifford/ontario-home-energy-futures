# Conflicts of Interest

## Current disclosure

As of this version, Ontario Home Energy Futures:

- Receives **no funding** from any solar installer, battery manufacturer,
  EV charger manufacturer, financing provider, energy retailer, or
  aggregator.
- Has **no partnership or referral arrangement** with any installer or
  contractor.
- Contains **no affiliate links** anywhere in its templates, documentation,
  or generated output.
- Does **not sell or share** household leads, inputs, or results with any
  third party.
- Has **no sponsor** with the ability to influence a default assumption,
  a pre-selected scenario, or any comparative framing.

This is a factual statement about the project's current state, not a
promise that can silently expire — see the standing commitment in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) "No undisclosed vendor funding."

## If this changes

Any future funding, partnership, sponsorship, or material relationship
with a company whose products or services this tool models or compares
(solar installers, battery/EV manufacturers, financing providers,
aggregators, or utilities) must be:

1. Disclosed in this document before it takes effect, including the
   funder's identity, the nature and (where applicable) amount of
   support, and the specific scope of the relationship.
2. Structurally prevented from influencing any default assumption value,
   any scenario's pre-selection, any "recommended" framing, or any
   comparative ranking — see [MODEL_CARD.md](MODEL_CARD.md) "Prohibited
   use," which explicitly forbids this tool being used as a sales tool.
3. Reviewed against [docs/RED-TEAM-REVIEW.md](docs/RED-TEAM-REVIEW.md)'s
   consumer-advocate section for any new risk the relationship introduces.

## Individual contributor disclosures

Individual contributors with a personal or professional relationship to a
company whose products this tool models (e.g., employment at a solar
installer or utility) should disclose that relationship on any pull
request touching assumptions, scenario defaults, or comparative
methodology, even where no project-level funding is involved.

## How to report a suspected conflict

Open a GitHub issue, or if the concern involves a maintainer directly, use
the general contact path described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
