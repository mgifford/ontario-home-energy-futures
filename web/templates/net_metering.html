Solar and net metering

Ontario net metering does not pay you cash for exported solar electricity. It creates a non-cash generation credit that can only offset the eligible electricity-energy charge on future imported electricity, and that credit expires 12 months after it is created if unused.

Oversized systems can waste credits

A system sized well above household consumption will export more than it can ever use as credit within the 12-month window, producing expired, wasted credit. This tool does not hide that outcome.

Group purchase discount

A 5% or 10% neighbourhood group-purchase discount (or a custom value) applies only to eligible quote components — panels, inverter, racking, electrical equipment, labour, and installer overhead. It never automatically discounts government fees, taxes, permitting, utility connection charges, or financing.
