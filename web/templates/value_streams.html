Status definitions

Current
Available to eligible Ontario households today.
Closed to new participants
Existing contracts may continue, but new participants cannot join.
Pilot
Limited to selected participants, locations, or equipment.
Proposed
Under formal Ontario consideration but not generally available.
Demonstrated elsewhere
Operating outside Ontario, not (yet) available here.
Illustrative scenario
A user-defined hypothetical future, not a real programme.

Preventing double counting

The same electricity or grid service is never compensated twice unless a programme's rules expressly permit it. For example, solar used directly in the home cannot also be exported and receive a net-metering credit — these are mutually exclusive fates for the same kWh. Every such rule is recorded in a machine-readable compatibility matrix and enforced in code, not left to chance. See assumptions/value_stream_compatibility.yaml and Methodology.
