Ontario Home Energy Futures

An open, Ontario-specific tool for exploring what a range of possible electricity costs over the next 10 to 30 years could look like for your household, with or without solar.

This is a planning tool, not a prediction

Every number on this site comes from a documented, editable assumption. Nothing here is a guaranteed future price or a promised saving. See About and limitations.

What this answers

Given current Ontario electricity costs, documented trends, and your household's plans, what range of costs could you face over the next 20 years, with or without solar?

Compare

  • Staying on the grid without solar
  • Buying solar now, or waiting one to five years
  • Buying individually, or with a 5% or 10% neighbourhood group-purchase discount
  • Paying cash or financing
  • Your current consumption, and future consumption after an EV, heat pump, electric water heater, or added cooling
  • Low, reference, high, and custom electricity-price scenarios
  • Flat, moderate, faster, and custom solar-cost-decline scenarios

Get started

  1. Describe your household — pick a standard bill or enter your own consumption.
  2. Add a solar quote — or use a labelled regional estimate.
  3. Compare buying now, waiting, cash, and financing.
  4. Review the assumptions behind every number.
