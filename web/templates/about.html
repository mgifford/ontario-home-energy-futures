About and limitations

What this application does

Ontario Home Energy Futures helps Ontario households explore a range of possible electricity costs over the next 10 to 30 years, with or without solar, using documented, editable assumptions.

What this application does not do

  • It does not predict future electricity prices or solar costs.
  • It does not guarantee savings, payback periods, or investment returns.
  • It does not treat provincial demand-growth forecasts as retail-price forecasts.
  • It does not treat every generated solar kWh as having the same financial value.
  • It does not assume solar eliminates fixed delivery charges.
  • It does not treat exported energy as a cash payment.
  • It does not require an account or store your household bill data on a server.
  • It does not require an exact street address.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or behavioural profiling.
  • It is not financial advice.

Relationship to neighbourhood-solar

This project grew out of discussion in mgifford/neighbourhood-solar#4. neighbourhood-solar remains a separate, deliberately simple, static, JavaScript-free organizing site for neighbourhood group-purchase initiatives; this project does not change its architecture or scope.
