Battery versus generator

Full battery and generator financial modelling is deferred to Phase 3 of this project, so that Phase 1's data provenance and net-metering accuracy are not weakened by rushing dispatch modelling ahead of validated hourly load and generation data. This page documents the intended comparison structure and its illustrative reference figures.

What will be compared

  • Solar alone
  • Solar plus battery
  • Battery backup versus a gasoline or natural-gas generator
  • Energy savings versus resilience benefits — kept separate, never combined

Resilience is reported separately from savings

This project does not assign an invented dollar value to outage resilience by default. When battery/generator modelling is implemented, results will separately report: critical load in watts, estimated backup duration, maximum supported load, fuel dependence, automatic vs. manual operation, whether solar can recharge a battery during an outage, and any assumptions about grid-forming or islanding equipment.
